## Synthesis for our study

- **No paper varies perturbation diversity at a fixed corruption volume.** [1] and [3] sweep volume; [1] always mixes all six types and [3] never mixes. Our Mixed-3 vs single-family comparison at 50% corruption is not covered by any of them.
- **No text paper tests on held-out perturbation families.** Only [6] (vision, adversarial) has a cross-type transfer grid. Its result, that transfer is limited and can be negative, is the main prior reason to expect that Mixed-3 might *not* help on unseen families. That makes a null result informative.
- **Direction of prior effects.** Input-side text noise in training is usually harmless or mildly helpful for clean accuracy ([1], [3]). Label noise is harmful ([3]), which supports our choice of label-preserving families. Embedding noise helps answer quality ([4]) but has never been tested for robustness to text noise. That makes `shared/neftune` a meaningful control.
- **Scale.** [3] is the only paper with a ≤1B model (Qwen2-0.5B), and [1] finds that small models gain less consistently from noisy tuning. Treat this as a reason to run the optional 1.5B scale check.
